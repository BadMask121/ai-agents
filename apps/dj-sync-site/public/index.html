Three steps, about five minutes

No terminal and nothing to configure. DJ Sync sets up the tools it needs by itself.

  1. Install DJ Sync

    Drag it to Applications and open it. It downloads its own tools and backs up your Rekordbox library before it changes anything.

  2. Connect your phone or assistant

    Pair Telegram with a QR code, or paste your private link into Claude or ChatGPT once. Free options included, and it works from your phone.

  3. Just ask

    “Download this into Friday Warm-up.” “Make a playlist of my 120 BPM house.” “Rename Sat Night to Saturday.”
