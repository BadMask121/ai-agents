Connect iPhone

Add DJ Sync to your iPhone’s Share menu in one scan. Share a song from Spotify or YouTube, pick a playlist, done. Free, no account.

This uses Apple’s Shortcuts app, so it’s for iPhone and iPad. On Android, use Telegram.

  1. Scan the code on your Mac

    Click the DJ Sync icon in your menu bar, go to Assistants and click Connect iPhone. DJ Sync makes your own Shortcut and shows a QR code. Point your iPhone’s camera at it and tap the link that appears.

    The code works for 10 minutes. If it runs out, click New QR.

  2. Tap Add Shortcut

    A DJ Sync page opens on your iPhone. Tap Add Shortcut, then Add Shortcut again in the Shortcuts app. It’s called DJ Sync and already knows how to reach your Mac, so there’s nothing to type.

  3. Share a song

    In Spotify or YouTube, tap Share (you may need More), choose DJ Sync, then pick a playlist. A notification confirms it was sent. The track downloads on your Mac into that Rekordbox playlist. If your Mac is asleep or offline, it waits and runs as soon as it’s back.

    The first time, iPhone asks whether DJ Sync may connect to djsync.jeffrey.build. Tap Always Allow.

Your Mac needs to be signed into iCloud (System Settings → Apple Account): macOS signs the Shortcut with your Apple ID so iPhone can add it. If it can’t, DJ Sync shows your key and URL instead, and you can build the Shortcut yourself (below).
The Shortcut holds your private key. Don’t share it. If your phone is lost, choose New Key for iPhone in DJ Sync: the old Shortcut stops working, and you scan the new code to add a fresh one.

Advanced: build it yourself

Build the Shortcut by hand (if your Mac can’t make it)

In DJ Sync, open Assistants → Connect iPhone → Show key and URL and copy the key to your iPhone (Universal Clipboard or AirDrop). Then open the Shortcuts app, tap +, name it DJ Sync, and add these actions in order. Wherever it says Bearer YOUR-KEY, type the word Bearer, a space, then paste your key.

  1. Receive input from the Share Sheet. Tap the shortcut’s ⓘ, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and let it receive URLs and Text.
  2. Get Contents of URL (your playlists): URL https://djsync.jeffrey.build/api/playlists, method GET. Under Headers, add Authorization with the value Bearer YOUR-KEY.
  3. Get Dictionary Value: the Value for names in Contents of URL. It’s your default playlist first, then every playlist as Folder/Name.
  4. Choose from List: choose from Dictionary Value, with the prompt Add to which playlist?
  5. Get Contents of URL (send it): URL https://djsync.jeffrey.build/api/downloads, method POST, the same Authorization header, and Request Body: JSON with two fields:
    • text (Text) = Shortcut Input
    • playlist_name (Text) = Chosen Item
  6. Get Dictionary Value: the Value for message in Contents of URL.
  7. Show Notification: title DJ Sync, body Dictionary Value.

DJ Sync finds the links in whatever the Share Sheet sends, and the full Folder/Name picks exactly one playlist, even if two share a name.
