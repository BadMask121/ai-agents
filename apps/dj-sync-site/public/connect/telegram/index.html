Connect Telegram

Send links to the DJ Sync bot on Telegram and they land in Rekordbox. Free, on any phone, with no AI subscription.

  1. Pair Telegram with your Mac

    Click the DJ Sync icon in your menu bar, go to Assistants and click Connect Telegram. DJ Sync shows a QR code.

    • On your phone: scan the QR code with the camera. It opens Telegram.
    • Or on your Mac: click Open Telegram if you use Telegram there.

    Tap Start in the chat with the DJ Sync bot. The bot says hello, and the DJ Sync menu shows Telegram (@your-name).

    The code works once, for 10 minutes. If it expired, click New code in the same place. Pairing again from another chat moves DJ Sync to that chat.

  2. Send a link

    Paste a Spotify or YouTube link into the chat, or share it straight from the Spotify or YouTube app: Share → Telegram → DJ Sync. Add a playlist name after the link to choose where it goes:

    https://open.spotify.com/track/… gqom

    Names don’t need to be exact. If it isn’t clear which playlist you mean, or you didn’t say, the bot shows buttons: the closest playlists, Default (ChatGPT Downloads), and New playlist with the name you typed. Tap one.

  3. Get told when it’s done

    The bot messages you ✅ when the tracks are in Rekordbox, or ⚠️ with the reason if something failed. If your Mac is asleep or offline, the request waits and runs as soon as it’s back.

Commands

  • /playlists lists your playlists with track counts. Add words to filter, like /playlists afro.
  • /search followed by words finds tracks in your library, like /search burna boy.
  • /status shows your last few downloads and changes.
  • /help shows what the bot can do.
  • /stop disconnects this chat from your Mac. You can also click Disconnect next to Telegram in the DJ Sync menu.

What the bot stores is in the privacy policy.
